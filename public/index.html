      ⚡ The Series

      Under the new Electricity Act, the CEB is being restructured into four separate entities. Each webinar takes one of them, with an industry expert explaining how it works and what it means for the next generation of engineers.

      01Sun · 15 Nov
      EGL

      Electricity Generation Lanka

      How Sri Lanka produces its electricity: hydro, thermal and the fast-growing renewables, and the role of generation companies in the new structure.

      02Date TBA
      NTNSP

      National Transmission Network Service Provider

      The high-voltage backbone that carries power from the plants across the country, and how it must grow for a renewable future.

      03Date TBA
      EDL

      Electricity Distribution Lanka

      From the substation to homes and industries: how power reaches every customer, and how rooftop solar is changing the network.

      04Date TBA
      NSO

      National System Operator

      The control room of the nation: balancing supply and demand every second to keep the whole grid stable.
